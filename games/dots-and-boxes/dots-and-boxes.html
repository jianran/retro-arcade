How to play
  • 🎲Roll & draw. Each turn you get 30 seconds: both dice roll, you add them, and you click any open line whose number equals your sum.
  • 🔢Numbered board. The 5×7 grid's lines are numbered 2–12, weighted like real dice odds.
  • 🔳Close a square. Draw the 4th side of a square to claim it: +1 point, locked forever.
  • ⏰Too slow? No action in 30 seconds awards the next player +1 point.
  • ⏭️Skip. End your turn and give the next player +½ point. Halves combine: 2 halves = 1 point, 3 halves = 2 points (3s convert first; leftover single halves don't count at the end).
  • 💣Bomb ×3. Destroy one connected chain of an opponent's lines — every line of the same color connected to the one you click. A finished box that loses one of its sides reopens and gives its point back. Each bombed line has a fixed 75% chance to come back reusable, 25% to be destroyed for good (✕).
  • 🌩️One-time events. Once each per game, they strike at rare intervals (≥3 turns apart): 🏰 Billionaire Castle (+3 to a random player), ☀️ Drought (5 claimed lines vanish), 🍯 Honey Jar (honey covers the screen for 100 seconds), ⚡ Lightning (3 claimed lines destroyed for good), 🧲 Magnet (a random player steals 2 points), ⏳ Time Warp (+15 seconds this turn), 🌪️ Tornado (scrambles the numbers on all open lines), 🎡 Wheel of Fortune (spin — the chosen player wins +2 points and an extra bomb). Lines vanishing from a finished square reopen it and cost its point.
  • 🛡️Shield ×1. Blocks one bomb aimed at you. You get 10 seconds to choose — if you don't answer, the shield blocks automatically.
  • 🏆Winning. A random target of 15–20 points is set at the start. The first to reach it ends the game, and the higher total score wins (unconverted half-points excluded).

    • 🌩️One-time events strike at rare intervals: castles, droughts, honey jars, lightning, magnets, time warps, tornadoes and spinning wheels — each only once per game.
    • 🏆First to the 15–20 point target ends the game; highest total wins. Halves convert: 2 = 1 point, 3 = 2 points.
