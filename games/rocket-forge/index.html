How to play

Click a part in the bay, then click the rocket to place it. Engines need a fuel tank in their own stage; a decoupler splits the rocket into stages. Keep liftoff TWR ≥ 1.0 and mind your budget.

In flight you sit in the command pod, looking out: steer with ←→ / AD, thrust down with ↓ / S. Every burst burns RCS monopropellant — dodge hazards yourself, or press Q to let the AI autopilot dodge (it burns the same extra fuel).

SPACE launch  ·  SPACE jettison stage  ·  P parachute  ·  ESC pause  ·  Q AI  ·  1–7 time warp (1–100×)  ·  ⌫ sell part (press, then click it)  ·  ⌘Z undo  ·  M mute

Goal: cross the Kármán line at 100 km. The parachute is manual only — press P (or the CHUTE button) on the way down, before you hit the ground. Land slower than 12 m/s to survive.

The sky is not empty: meteorites, space junk, storm cells, birds, and even other rockets can damage your hull. Keep the HULL gauge above 0% — impacts light up a red warning.

Speed makes heat: the engine and fast air (Max Q, reentry) push the TEMP gauge up. A Heat Shield part mounted directly under the pod gives an ablative buffer — it burns away above 700 °C instead of your hull. Without one, critical heat hits the hull directly; the parachute melts at 1,050 °C and the rocket breaks up at 1,300 °C. Deploy the chute early on a hot descent.
